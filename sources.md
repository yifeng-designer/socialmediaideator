# Source Pool

Use sources as signals, not as text to paraphrase. Never copy hooks, captions, or proprietary claims.

## Breathwork and nervous-system education

- `thebreathworkmovement`: breathwork education, practical exercises, emotional regulation
- `breathwrk`: accessible breathing routines and use cases
- `kaspersfocus`: breath, focus, performance, and everyday regulation

## Product and category context

- `glimp.health`: brand voice, product use, customer language
- `moonbird.life`: handheld breathing guidance and category framing
- `somnox.sleep`: sleep routines, recovery, and calming product context

## Broader wellbeing language

- `headspace`: meditation, stress, sleep, everyday mindfulness
- `calm`: cultural moments, emotional wellbeing, sleep, relaxation

## Source-mix rule

For a weekly batch, draw from at least three accounts and at least two source groups. No single account should drive more than 40% of the ideas.
