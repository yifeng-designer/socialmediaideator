# Image Prompt Guideline

## Visual character

Warm editorial realism, natural domestic spaces, tactile materials, quiet imperfection, believable bodies and gestures, restrained color, and enough visual stillness for social copy.

## Required prompt ingredients

- subject and specific action
- environment and time of day
- camera distance, angle, and framing
- light quality and palette
- emotional tone and physical cues
- negative-space placement
- aspect ratio, normally `4:5`
- exclusions that prevent generic wellness stock imagery

## Product fidelity

When Glimp is shown, preserve the real device design. Describe its location, orientation, scale, grip, visible hand count, and which fingers touch it. Ask for no duplicate hands, no substitute object, and no altered logo or controls.

## Default exclusions

No spa setting, yoga cliche, luxury retreat, exaggerated smile, pristine showroom, floating objects, duplicated limbs, malformed hands, fake text, or overly polished commercial lighting.
